A CORNER
SHOP THAT

SCALE.

Imayam started as a single shelf inside a family-run kirana in T. Nagar. Today it's a six-shop network across Tamil Nadu — still family-run, still allergic to compromise.

Every grain, jar and bottle on our shelf is traceable to a single farmer. We pay them directly. We pay them fairly. The food tastes like food.
